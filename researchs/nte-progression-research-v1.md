# Neverness to Everness — Endgame Guide
### Daily · Weekly · Monthly Plan + Currencies & Farming Zones

> **Reset times:** Daily = 5 AM server time (EU: GMT+2 / Asia: GMT+8 / US: GMT-5) · Weekly = Monday 5 AM · Monthly = 1st of each month

---

## Priority Legend
- 🔵 **Critical** — do first, never skip
- 🟢 **High priority** — do every session
- 🟡 **Medium priority** — do when time allows
- ⚪ **Optional / Conditional** — situational value

## ✅ Daily Checklist

### 🔵 1. Spend All Character Pixels (Stamina)
- Regenerates at **1 every 6 minutes** (240/day base cap)
- Cap can be raised by buying the **Eden Apartment** and upgrading the **Dream Weaver's Knot** furniture
- **Never let stamina hit cap** — it stops generating
- Always run the **highest Rabbit Hole difficulty** you can clear
- At **Hunter Level 30+**: S-rank Cartridges and Hard Rewind unlock
- At **Hunter Level 50**: highest Anomaly Zone difficulty unlocks
- Use the **Exploration Guide compass** to track and fast-travel to the right zone

### 🔵 2. Claim Login Reward + Event Rewards
- Open the Event page and claim everything available
- Missing even one day breaks streak bonuses and loses daily premium currency
- Check for active **redeem codes** — each gives ~100 Annulith and expire without warning
- Codes are redeemable via Settings → Redeem Code (unlocks after Episode 0)

### 🔵 3. Complete Daily Quests (Exploration Guide)
- Open Exploration Guide → second tab → **Participation Tasks**
- Resets daily — rewards Fons, Beetle Coins, Annulith, and Dreamless Seeds
- Reaching **Activity Level 100** per day claims all premium daily rewards
- Spending your Character Pixels usually completes these tasks automatically

### 🔵 4. Circle Bounty (Battle Pass)
- Open Circle Bounty → claim EXP from completed daily tasks → collect level rewards
- Target: **12,000 Circle Bounty EXP per week**
- Logging in daily alone gives **7,000 EXP/week** from this menu
- The premium tier (Elite Hunter Supplies) is worth purchasing if you spend money

### 🟢 5. Manage Café by Origen
- Open City Tycoon → Café by Origen
- **Collect earnings** (4,800+ Fons per day baseline)
- Open Management → deselect least profitable recipes → select best ones
- Press **Restock** → choose 24h if still playing, 72h overnight, 72h if taking a break
- Café earns passively even while offline
- Do not over-buy ingredients early — menus upgrade as you level up

### 🟢 6. Nacupeda's Pool Blessing
- **Location:** West side of Bridge Crossing (Fountain icon on map)
- Do this **first if possible** — the buff boosts Fons income for the day
- **Daily reward:** 6–8 Mhm! Coins
- Accumulate **100 Mhm! Coins** → buy exclusive S-Rank Arc "Your Happiness is Priceless" from the Arc Shop (~12–17 days of daily visits)
- Choose **"Make a Sincere Wish"** for Mhm! Coins; the other option gives furniture items

### 🟢 7. Fortune Reading at The Witch's House (×3 daily)
- **Location:** Southwest of Hankaku Street (purple Crystal Ball icon on map)
- Completely **unmarked** — no in-game reminder exists
- You get **3 draws per day**, each giving one of three results:
  - **Bless** — daily combat buff (e.g. +20% Crit Damage) active for the rest of the day
  - **Treasure Hunt** — combat encounter → collect Oracle Stone → submit to Blackbird at Witch's House for milestone Annulith rewards
  - **Lost Tales** — short hidden quest for Hunter EXP + Easter Egg achievements

### 🟢 8. Give 10 Gifts to Characters
- **Daily limit:** 10 gifts total, max 3 per individual character
- Prioritize **Edgar first** — Rank 10 unlocks a weekly reward item
- Match gifts to each character for maximum Affection:
  - **Chiz:** Holy Worship Month (200 Affection) or Fantasia (200 Affection, cheaper)
  - Check each character's gift preferences for most efficient use of your daily cap
- **Fluffy Cotton** from your Compressed Boom Boom Cloud (drops every 2–3 days) gives 400 Affection to any character — give to whoever you're raising fastest

### 🟡 9. Farm Fons with Chiz
- **How:** Hold Chiz's Basic Attack against enemies to extract Fons
- **Daily cap:** 40,000 Fons
- **Total lifetime cap:** 250,000 Fons (raised to 500,000 at City Tycoon Level 28)
- Run this passively every day until the total cap is reached
- Chiz is obtainable for free via the City Tycoon level-up track

### 🟡 10. Claim Free Daily Module *(requires Hathor)*
- Requires Hathor from pulling or the S-Rank Selector
- Her Anomaly Furniture (Hamster Ball) unlocks one free Module per day
- **Routine:**
  1. City Tycoon → Property → Eden Apartment → Hamster Ball
  2. Click "Little Melon Seed" icon → press Source → Track
  3. GPS tracker leads to Haunted Vending Machines on the map
  4. Collect ~24 Little Melon Seeds per day
  5. Return to Apartment → upgrade Hamster Ball to maximum possible level
  6. Claim the daily Module
- Higher level Hamster Ball = higher rarity Module chance
- In mid-game, Eden Apartment caps Hamster Ball at Level 6; higher apartments unlock higher levels

### 🟡 11. Character Date & Map Bond Encounters
- **Cinema / Ferris Wheel** (near Boss Macaon): +200 bond EXP daily with any invited character
- **Map bond encounters:** Up to 3 per day — character portraits appear on the map, approach for a bonding scene + bond EXP
- Affinity levels unlock character-specific skins

### ⚪ 12. Pray at Fortune Shades Tree
- **Location:** Yuanmu Hill (far east of map)
- Buy a Prayer Plaque from the nearby vendor first
- Optional affinity-building activity with minor passive rewards

### ⚪ 13. Bust Illegal Activities
- Open-world patrol activity
- Rewards Fons and side materials up to a daily limit
- Good to do passively while traveling to other objectives

## 📅 Weekly Checklist
> Resets every **Monday at 5 AM** server time

> ⚠️ **Missing weekly bosses is the single biggest account setback.** Hard cap of 3 attempts per week — missing any week permanently delays character skill upgrades.

### 🔵 1. Anomaly Pilgrimage — Weekly Bosses (×3 attempts)
- **Hard cap:** 3 boss fights per week — never skip
- Drops **Esper Skill upgrade materials**, the rarest endgame progression resource
- At launch there are 2 bosses available
- **Strategy:** Prioritize the boss dropping materials for the characters you are actively building
- If you are close to a new Appraisal Level mid-week, consider saving attempts until after leveling up for better drop quality

### 🔵 2. Spend All City Stamina
- City Stamina does **NOT** regenerate daily — it resets to full every Monday
- **Unspent Stamina at reset = permanently lost Fons**
- Fixed rate: **1 Stamina = 1,000 Fons** regardless of activity chosen
- City Stamina cap by Tycoon Level:
  - Level 5: 200 Stamina (200,000 Fons/week)
  - Level 10: 350 Stamina (350,000 Fons/week)
  - Level 16: 500 Stamina (500,000 Fons/week)
  - Level 23: 700 Stamina (700,000 Fons/week)
- **Fastest activity for Fons:** City Delivery
- **Best for Beetle Coins:** Fishing (unlocks at Level 10+)
- Activities: Deliveries, Racing, Barista mini-game, Taxi mode — same Fons per stamina for all

### 🔵 3. Pink Paws Heist (1,000,000 Fons cap)
- **Resets every 2 weeks** (not weekly — check your cap counter)
- Cap: **1,000,000 Fons** per cycle
- Does **NOT** cost City Stamina — pure bonus income on top of everything else
- Average run: ~150,000 Fons in 9 minutes + 600 Popper + 950 Pink Paws Credits
- **Tips for efficient runs:**
  - Follow the fixed spawn route — don't wander
  - Avoid unnecessary combat — every HP loss costs Fons
  - Only grab pickups along the natural path
  - Extract when the **'!' marker** appears — no marker means the run is lost
  - Never push past the timer — full run is lost if you do
  - Don't open forced safes before clearing the 2 guards beside them
  - Start extracting with ~2 minutes left if cautious, ~30 seconds if you know the route
  - Start with ~2 min remaining if cautious, ~30s if experienced

### 🟢 4. Special Delivery Commission (Old Mailbox)
- **Location:** Old Mailbox at Eden Apartment (also in City Delivery menu — look for the commission with zero stamina cost)
- Rewards Fons **scaled to your current level** at NO City Stamina cost
- Upgrade the Old Mailbox to increase payout
- Do this even after City Stamina is fully exhausted — it's extra free Fons

### 🟢 5. Realm of Greed / Mammon Boss
- **Requirements:** Property (Wiener Apartment) + Blind Mammon Plushie furniture
- **Setup:** Before fighting, check the Auction House for Gluttonous Eyes and Covetous Coins to upgrade Blind Mammon — higher upgrades = more Fons
- **Routine:** Go to your apartment → interact with Blind Mammon → teleport to Realm of Greed → defeat boss
- **Rewards:** Up to 100,000 Fons per week (scales with your level — don't rush it at low level)

### 🟢 6. Ebisu's Auction House
- **Unlocks:** After completing Chapter 3 of the Main Story
- **No popup or reminder** — you must visit manually every week
- **Location:** New Herland District, under the Never-ending Arachna Anomaly Pilgrimage (Auction House icon on map)
- **How to enter:** Teleport to Auction House icon → talk to Iris → Go to Auction → talk to Iori
- **Bidding strategy:** Let all NPCs bid until only one remains, then place your bid (saves repeated 200 Fons failed bids)
- **Priority items to buy:**
  1. Gluttonous Eye (Blind Mammon upgrade)
  2. Covetous Coins (Blind Mammon upgrade)
  3. Other rare character upgrade materials if budget allows

### 🟢 7. Anomaly Commissions (Full Set)
- Completing a full set rewards **15,000 Fons + side character materials**
- Each individual commission gives 10–60 Annulith on first completion
- Clearing milestone commission counts gives bonus Annulith
- One free S-Rank Arc is awarded at specific milestone completions
- Weekly repeatable runs contribute to ongoing Annulith income

### 🟡 8. Beyond the Rails — Special Routes
- **Location:** Train station, northern Miguel District
- **Enemies:** ~Level 60 — wait until your characters are well-developed
- Unlock Special Routes by clearing All-Day Routes first (All-Day Routes are always available)
- Special Routes reset every 14 days (per game version update)
- **10 stops per cycle, 3 objectives per stop** = up to 30 Platform Seals
- Every **3 Platform Seals** = Annulith + Train Log rewards
- One of the best recurring Annulith sources in endgame
- Push as far as possible before patch maintenance for the new version

### 🟡 9. Guild Boss
- Weekly guild boss fight with guild members
- Missing it wastes guild contributions and drops
- Check the guild schedule in-game and coordinate with members

### 🟡 10. Field Boss
- Weekly field boss for boss materials and progression resources
- Check the boss schedule in-game
- Scales with Appraisal Level — higher level = more rewards

## 🗓️ Monthly Checklist
> Resets on the **1st of each month**

> 💡 Set a calendar reminder for the 1st — missing a cycle loses Solid Dice and Tri-Keys that don't carry over.

### 🔵 1. Lost Exchange Shop — Buy Pulls First
- **Currency:** Lost Pieces (earned from duplicate character/Arc pulls)
- Has a **purchase limit** — restocks monthly, so spend before the reset
- **Priority buy order:**
  1. Solid Dice (Limited Banner pulls)
  2. Fabricated Dice (Standard Banner pulls)
  3. Tri-Keys (Arc weapon currency)
- **Do NOT buy Fons here** — price is heavily inflated vs. pull value
- If low on Lost Pieces, prioritize Dice over Tri-Keys (most S-Class Arcs are free in-game)

### 🟢 2. Warp Exchange Shop
- **Currency:** Warp Pieces (earned from duplicate pulls — no purchase limit)
- **No monthly reset** — but check monthly for smart spending decisions
- **Options (same Warp Piece cost for all):**
  - Save 720 for 1 standard character
  - 30 Limited Banner pulls
  - 120,000 Fons per pull-equivalent
- **Recommendation:** Save the decision until endgame meta is settled; priority depends on roster gaps vs. pull savings vs. Fons needs

### 🟢 3. Arc Selection Shop (Tri-Key Exchange)
- **Refreshes monthly**
- Uses Tri-Keys to buy Standard Character Arcs (same as free ones from Bosses/Anomaly Hunting)
- You can directly buy **any S-Class Arc at 25 Tri-Keys** — no RNG, completely deterministic
- Identify the best Arc for your main DPS and work toward the 25 Tri-Key direct purchase rather than rolling the Arc banner blind

### 🟡 4. Hunter Exchange Shop (Fons → Annulith)
- **Unlocks:** By raising Tycoon Level
- Allows purchasing Annulith with Fons
- Also stocks pool currency and character upgrade materials
- Only use after your Fons income is stable — don't buy Annulith here if your City Tycoon is underdeveloped

### 🟡 5. Beyond the Rails — Clear Before Patch
- Special Routes rotate with every new game version
- All Annulith from Platform Seals must be claimed **before server maintenance** for the new patch
- Ensure teams are fully built and push as far as possible at the end of each version cycle

### 🟡 6. Pink Paws Heist — Second Biweekly Reset
- Resets every 2 weeks, so there are roughly **2 cycles per month**
- 1,000,000 Fons cap per cycle
- Track your cap counter in-game to know when it resets

## ⚔️ Farming Zones — What to Farm Where

> Always run the **highest difficulty available**. Drop rates scale directly with difficulty. Don't seriously farm Cartridges until **Hunter Level 30**.

### 🔵 Rabbit Hole — Cartridges & Modules
**Location:** Cape Square, south side of New Herland District (high-rise rooftop — find the bunny NPC)  
**Cost:** 40–80 Character Pixels per run  
**Resets:** Unlimited daily (limited only by your stamina)

**What it drops:**
- **Cartridges** — gear with randomized stats and set bonuses (endgame gear system)
- **Carrota** (Bronze/Silver/Gold) — currency for Module Rewinds
- **Manhole EXP materials** — used to level up Modules

**Stages inside Rabbit Hole:** Sculpture Gallery, Latitude Loom, Defend the Radish, Mental Map, Night of the Rails, Clock Tricks — each drops specific Cartridge sets (e.g. Shadow Creed, Tiny Big Adventure)

**Module Rewind tiers:**
- **Easy Rewind** (Bronze Carrota) — fully random Module shapes, available from Level 10
- **Normal Rewind** (Silver Carrota) — pick 8 preferred shapes, unlocks at Level 20
- **Hard Rewind** (Gold Carrota) — legendary Modules, unlocks at Level 30

**Cartridge farming tips:**
- Don't upgrade past +5 until you verify sub-stats — if they don't fit your build, abandon and farm a better piece
- Sub-stats are visible before upgrading — you don't need to invest to preview them
- Focus on one character at a time — a fully-geared main carry outperforms several half-geared characters
- Activating 4-set bonuses is higher priority than perfect individual stats early on
- Fill every empty Module slot — empty cells produce no stats

### 🔵 Anomaly Pilgrimage — Esper Skill Upgrade Materials
**Resets:** 3 attempts per week (Monday reset)  
**What it drops:** Esper Ability upgrade materials — the rarest character progression resource  
**Note:** Hard 3-attempt cap per week. Prioritize the boss dropping materials for your current build targets. Time attempts around Appraisal Level-ups for better quality drops.

### 🟢 Beyond the Rails — Annulith + Train Logs
**Location:** Train station, northern Miguel District  
**Resets:** Every ~14 days per game version  
**Enemies:** ~Level 60 — requires well-developed teams  

**Structure:**
- Clear All-Day Routes first to unlock Special Routes
- 10 stops per Special Route cycle
- 3 objectives per stop = up to 30 Platform Seals per cycle
- Every 3 Platform Seals = Annulith + Train Logs
- Clear before server maintenance when a new patch drops

### 🟢 General Anomaly Zones — Character EXP & Arc Materials
**Cost:** Character Pixels  
**What drops:**
- **Hunter Guides** — character level-up EXP books
- **Unsolved Numerals** — skill upgrade materials
- **Arc Ascension materials** — Arc weapon upgrade components

Use the **Exploration Guide compass** to track which material you need and fast-travel to the correct zone. Always run the hardest difficulty you can clear.

### 🟡 Realm of Greed — Weekly Fons Boss
**Access:** Blind Mammon furniture at your Property (requires Gluttonous Eye from Auction House)  
**Resets:** Weekly (Monday)  
**Rewards:** Up to 100,000 Fons per week (scales with level)  
**Tip:** Upgrade Blind Mammon weekly via Auction House purchases to increase payout. Higher level = more Fons. Do NOT rush this at low level.

### 🟡 Oracle Stones (Open World)
**How to find:** Witch's House Treasure Hunt fortune pinpoints locations on the map  
**Reward:** Submit to Blackbird at The Witch's House for milestone Annulith rewards across all Divination Levels  
**Also:** Collect "Gift from 21" items scattered on the map for small Annulith amounts

## 🏗️ Character Upgrade Priority Order

1. **Character Level** — use Hunter Guides from Anomaly Zones, gated by Appraisal Level
2. **Esper Skills** — weekly boss materials from Anomaly Pilgrimage (hardest bottleneck)
3. **Arc (Weapon)** — Ascension materials from general Anomaly Zones; Arc itself via Tri-Keys
4. **Cartridges** — farm Rabbit Hole at Level 30+ for S-rank pieces
5. **Modules** — farm Carrota from Rabbit Hole → use Rewind (Hard at Level 30 for legendary)

> Beetle Coins are spent on both character leveling and Arc upgrading — collect your daily commission cap every day without fail.

## 💡 Key Tips & Mistakes to Avoid

- **Never let Character Pixels cap** — they stop generating at max. Spend daily.
- **Never let City Stamina carry over the weekly reset** — all unspent is lost.
- **Check the Auction House manually every week** — there is no reminder or popup.
- **Don't buy Fons from the Lost Exchange** — the rate is terrible compared to spending on pulls.
- **Don't farm Cartridges seriously before Hunter Level 30** — earlier pieces become outdated quickly.
- **Don't upgrade Cartridges past +5 without checking sub-stats** — you can preview them without investing.
- **Pink Paws Heist resets every 2 weeks, not weekly** — don't confuse the cycle.
- **Save Solid Dice for Limited Banners** — the 100% guaranteed featured character (no 50/50) means every die spent is working toward what you want.
- **Tri-Key direct purchase at 25 for any S-Class Arc** — don't roll the Arc banner blind when you can buy the exact Arc you need.
- **Café ingredients** — don't over-buy early. Menus upgrade as you level, and excess ingredients for old recipes go to waste.
- **Realm of Greed scales with your level** — higher level = more Fons. Don't rush it at low level.
